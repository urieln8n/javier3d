# Product Strategy

## Primary goal
Generate qualified leads for professional pizza ovens.

## Core customer journeys
1. Restaurant/pizzeria owner needs a new oven → explores models → requests quote.
2. Entrepreneur opening a pizzeria → discovers full setup opportunity → requests consultation.
3. Existing lead sent by Javier → receives premium product explanation → progresses toward sale.

## Value proposition
**Explore the oven before you buy it.**
The 3D layer should make construction, finishes and product differences easier to understand.

## Future cross-sell
PedidoAI may later be offered as a separate or bundled digital-ordering solution. Keep its technical application separate; connect commercially via landing/CTA, not a monorepo merge.
