# Premium industrial + Mediterranean + cinematic

Planning only. No current UI changed. Commercial goal: help a pizzeria owner understand a real JAVA oven and submit a qualified quote request.

## Visual principles

- Warm charcoal background; warm ivory text; restrained terracotta/red accents. Proposed palette: charcoal #171411, ivory #F4EEE5, terracotta #B9573C. Validate contrast before implementation; colors are design proposals, not brand approvals.
- Generous spacing, strong hierarchy and large editorial typography. Short product-led headlines paired with a quiet, readable UI sans. Start with existing system fonts; evaluate licensed, self-hosted subsets before adding fonts.
- Real product photography supplied by Javier. Do not modify originals or use reference crops as approved launch photography. AI/concept references remain labeled marketing concepts.
- Real, faithful 3D oven as the visual hero. Geometry, finishes and construction must match approved evidence. No decorative 3D feature without a comprehension or purchase purpose.
- Minimal chrome; avoid adding panels around everything. Use product scale, negative space, type and light to create quality.
- No generic SaaS gradients, neon AI styling, excessive glassmorphism, template-like card grids or over-animation. Current gold accents, badges and gradients are existing starter choices, not this final system.

## Commercial hierarchy

Product → clear value → request quote. Primary action: SOLICITAR PRESUPUESTO. Secondary: EXPLORAR EN 3D. Do not make visitors understand the technology to understand the oven. Avoid copy aimed at selling web development services; the current starter mixes these audiences.

Credibility must come from approved facts, original images and real support/contact information. Certifications, warranty, capacity, heat performance, materials and prices remain pending unless verified. Do not use invented badges, testimonials or statistics.

## Proposed scales for a later phase

Use a 4/8 px spacing rhythm, 24–48 px component gaps and 64–128 px section spacing on desktop; reduce spacing on mobile without shrinking controls. Headline proposal: 64–96 px desktop, 48–64 px tablet, 40–52 px mobile; body 16–20 px, comfortable line height. Tune by actual Spanish copy rather than forcing these ranges.

Subtle UI transitions around 160–240 ms; a single restrained entrance can reach 700 ms. Motion must preserve immediate reading and CTA access. Reduced motion means a fully composed static first frame and no automatic camera/flame movement. No scroll locking, scroll hijacking or mandatory cinematic sequence.

## Review approach

UI UX Pro Max may guide hierarchy and interaction during a future design phase. 21st.dev is optional research, never a mandate to import a template. Use Vercel Guidelines for keyboard/focus, forms, contrast, touch and motion review; Impeccable for a final visual critique. Tool references and honest installation status are in STACK.md. Scroll Craft remains deferred.

Acceptance: feels like a high-end product launch, keeps the oven and quote path clear on all three viewports, uses truthful product content and remains understandable without animation or WebGL. This document is direction for review, not approval to implement.
